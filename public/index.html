About DeepTech

A software team you can rely on, before and after launch

DeepTech is an IT software development and maintenance company. We design, build and run AI applications, Android apps and web apps for businesses that want working software, not slide decks.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to what clients ask us first.

What kind of projects do you take on?

AI applications (chatbots, document search, voice agents, computer vision), Android apps, web apps and business websites, plus ongoing maintenance of existing software.

Can you maintain software someone else built?

Yes. We review the code first, fix urgent issues, then agree a maintenance plan covering bug fixes, upgrades, security patches and monitoring.

How long does a project take?

A focused web or mobile app usually takes 4 to 12 weeks. AI prototypes can be ready for testing in 2 to 3 weeks. You get an estimate in the proposal.

How is pricing decided?

After a short discovery call we send a fixed-scope proposal with milestones. For long-running work we also offer monthly retainers.

Who owns the code?

You do. Source code, designs and accounts are handed over to you at the end of the project.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We work remotely with clients worldwide and use shared channels and weekly demos to keep everyone in step.
